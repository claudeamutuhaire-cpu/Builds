Cookies Policy

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function and can improve your experience.

How HuntArts uses cookies

We use only essential cookies required for basic site functionality (for example, remembering your cookie consent preference). We do not currently use advertising or advanced tracking cookies.

Managing cookies

When you first visit the site you will see a cookie consent banner. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can also clear cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Third-party services

Our site may load fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from Tailwind CDN. These services may set their own cookies according to their privacy policies.
