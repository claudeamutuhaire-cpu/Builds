Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Free Sample Edits

Free sample edits (up to 2 per day after sign-up) are provided free of charge and are non-refundable as no payment is taken.

2. Paid Projects

For paid work:

  • If work has not started, a full refund may be requested within 48 hours of payment.
  • If work has begun, refunds are assessed on a case-by-case basis and may be partial depending on the stage of completion.
  • Completed and delivered work is generally non-refundable, unless there is a clear quality issue that we are unable to correct.

3. How to Request a Refund

Contact us at claudeamutuhaire@gmail.com or WhatsApp +256 754 595 667 with your project details and reason for the request. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

4. Processing

Approved refunds will be returned via the original payment method where possible, or an agreed alternative. Processing times depend on the payment provider.
